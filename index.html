Advanced Nutrition for Aquaculture

At NEWGEN BIOSCIENCE, we provide innovative nutritional solutions for fish and shrimp farming. Our products are developed to enhance growth, improve feed conversion, and support disease resistance, ensuring healthy aquatic stocks and sustainable aquaculture operations.

SporoGEN™ AQUA

High-concentration new generation probiotics

Unique blend of high-concentration new generation probiotics with Bacillus licheniformis, Bacillus pumilus, prebiotics, and yeast for pond water quality and Vibrio control.

Spark™ -2X

Highly bioavailable functional nutrient

Highly bioavailable functional nutrient essential for sustained growth and peak production, rich in amino acids, micronutrients, yeast, and pre-digested nutrients.

Opti-pH™

Synergistic combination of buffered organic acids and essential oils

Synergistic combination of buffered organic acids and essential oils to inhibit pathogenic bacteria in the shrimp gut and promote beneficial bacteria colonization.

Oxifi™

Oxygen-releasing water treatment

Formulation of sodium percarbonate and peroxyhydrate compounds with buffering agents and stabilizers that release active oxygen (minimum 13.0%).

Inocel™

Immunity & health enhancer

Synergistic formulation of beta-glucans, selenobiotics, sulphated polysaccharides, vitamin C, and CoQ10 to enhance shrimp immune responses and reduce stress.
